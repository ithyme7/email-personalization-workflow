# Landing Page Copy

## Headline

Evidence-backed cold email personalization, without the AI mush.

## Subheadline

Turn lead lists into reviewed opening lines grounded in real company research, source evidence, and quality checks.

## Problem

Most AI personalization sounds plausible but generic. It praises companies, invents context, or writes lines that could apply to any business.

## Solution

This workflow researches first, extracts evidence second, writes third, and reviews before delivery.

## Core Benefits

- Faster lead research.
- Fewer generic openers.
- Source-backed evidence for every line.
- Manual-review flags for weak rows.
- Tone profiles per customer or campaign.
- Excel output that is easy to inspect and edit.

## Best For

- outbound agencies
- founders doing targeted outreach
- growth teams
- sales teams running small high-quality campaigns

## Call To Action

Send a sample lead list and get a calibration batch.
