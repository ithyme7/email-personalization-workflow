# PROJECT_CONTEXT.md

## Project Name

Email Personalization Workflow

## Current Business Context

The project started from a real outbound personalization client case.

The client wants short, specific, human-sounding cold email openers that fit into a template around helping mobile app teams understand where users drop off and why.

The early client feedback showed that generic AI output is not enough. People can smell Ai generated content from miles away, if people know it can hurt the business that's why the focus lays on being as human as possible. You DO NOT want to hurt the clients business by bad output

The system must produce lines based on:

- specific friction
- right surface
- real evidence
- commercial implication
- natural pitch bridge
- human tone

---

## Current Client Feedback Rules

The client prefers:

- short conversational lines
- app/review evidence when available
- friction points over praise
- specific conversion/drop-off implications
- lowercase company names in opener copy
- no em dashes
- no fake app usage claims
- 2-3 opener options per company
- all original input columns preserved
- reviewable evidence and options

The client dislikes:

- blog-based commentary
- generic suggestions
- vague UX advice
- "I downloaded the app"
- fake familiarity
- long paragraphs
- lines that do not flow into the pitch
- wrong-surface observations
- output files that drop input columns

---

## Example Pitch Context

The personalized line usually flows into a pitch like:

```text
We help mobile app teams with this type of work, figure out where users drop off and why.
```

Therefore the opener should ideally connect to:

- user drop-off
- activation
- onboarding
- booking friction
- conversion
- retention
- trust
- proof
- unclear next action
- app review complaints

## Important Product Insight

The product is not only an AI writer.

It is a quality-controlled outbound workflow.

The valuable layers are:

- evidence collection
- angle selection
- multi-shot alternatives
- QC
- review
- calibration
- goldset/evals
- client-safe delivery
- feedback loops

---

## Commercial Positioning of the software you're building

Use this positioning:

```text
I build AI-assisted outbound workflows that turn messy lead data into enriched, evidence-backed personalization with QC, review loops and campaign-ready exports.
```

Avoid positioning as:

```text
I write AI cold emails.
I scrape leads.
I am a cheap VA.
```
